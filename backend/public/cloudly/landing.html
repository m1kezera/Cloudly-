FAQ

Quick answers. For anything else, ask the assistant in the corner.

How is my data protected?
Files in transit use TLS 1.3. At rest, data is encrypted with AES‑256. MFA is available and admins can enforce it on team plans.
Do you offer SSO and data residency?
Yes — on Enterprise contracts we support SSO (SAML/SCIM) and data residency options. Ask the assistant to reach sales.
Refund policy
Monthly plans refundable within 7 days; annual plans within 30 days on a pro‑rated basis.
